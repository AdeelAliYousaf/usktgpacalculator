# 🎓 USKT GPA Calculator

> A powerful, fun, and user-friendly GPA & CGPA calculator made specifically for students of the **University of Sialkot (USKT)**.  
Hosted at 👉 [usktgpacalculator](https://adeelaliyousaf.github.io/usktgpacalculator)

---

## 🧠 Features

- ✅ Calculate **SGPA** based on subject-wise marks and credit hours  
- ✅ Calculate **CGPA** using past semester data  
- ✅ Suggests the **best course to repeat** to maximize CGPA  
- ✅ Tells you what grade you need to achieve your **target CGPA**  
- ✅ **Offline support** – works as a **PWA**  
- ✅ Fun **sound effects** on GPA milestones (🎉 surprise sounds!)  
- ✅ Clean, modern UI with **USKT branding**  
- ✅ Mobile & desktop responsive
